Welcome,
I’m Hannah!

My foundation is visual communication, hierarchy, and accessibility. I don't stick to one lane. I love working across graphic design, UI/UX, and motion. I'm always curious about the systems and processes behind a project, and the best way to actually get the job done.

I pay attention to how design impacts people, what helps and what gets in the way. Usually that means the same few things: can someone actually use this, does it make sense at a glance, is anyone being left out. I love putting the puzzle pieces together and solving problems. I'd rather take feedback, learn from it, and adapt than perform like I already know what's best. If something can work better, I'll say so, and I'll help fix it.

Experienced Designer

5+ years of professional design experience across government, agencies, and freelance. Grounded in graphic design, growing into product and UX.

Collaborative by Nature

I thrive in teams where ideas are shared freely and feedback is treated as a tool. To me, great design comes from curiosity and open communication.

Continuous Learner

I like staying close to what's happening in design and tech. Right now that means sharpening my process, going deeper on UX, and figuring out where AI actually earns a place in the work.

Accessibility Focused

Good design should work for everyone. I do my best to build accessibility in from the start, not bolt it on at the end.
