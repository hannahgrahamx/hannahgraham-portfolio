### OVERVIEW

Sexual health is a topic that makes people uncomfortable before you’ve even said anything. The challenge wasn’t just what to communicate, but how to get people to actually stop and read it.

STI rates were climbing and the campaign needed to encourage safer sex across the province. Previous years had leaned into silly, lighthearted creative; I wanted something that could still be playful but carry enough weight to feel credible. A 70s-inspired visual language became the answer.

### APPROACH

**Make them do a double take.**

The goal was to stop someone mid-scroll and earn a second read when they landed on the copy. My job was to make sure the design set that moment up.

**01 — A 70s visual language**
Dark backgrounds, retro striped line work, a restrained palette with simple imagery. Playful enough to pull people in, considered enough to make the topic take it seriously.

**02 — Broad, but not generic**
The audience ran roughly 18 to 45, so the creative had to work across contexts. Anything clinical or preachy would only make people look away faster.

**03 — Bilingual & accessible**
Everything was produced in English and French, with English displayed primarily, and all colour choices tested to WCAG 2.1 Level AA.

**04 — Into film**
A filmed awareness ad carried the same system into live action. The colour palette to typographic hierarchy all matched my direction. Watch it for yourself here in ENGLISH or FRENCH.
